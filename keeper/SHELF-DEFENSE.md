# Shelf Defense · Keeper 0.11b

Open **Shelf Defense** in the Keeper sidebar. The page opens in the same tab and offers **Back to Keeper** and **Idle shelves**.

## Defend the stories

Choose Pencil Patrol, Sticky Situation, Rubber Ranger, Pocket Knight or Laser Lamp and place it beside the trail. Survive twenty waves in the Reading Room, Greenhouse and Midnight Archive. Each defender has an upgrade web; the waveform attack lab lets you shape its sound and attack.

The battle game saves its shelves in this browser. Existing valid ShelfDEFENSE saves on the same browser address keep their original format. Saves from another site, file or browser address are separate and are not automatically imported. Reloaded battles start paused; leaving the page stops its battle and audio. Sound requires your choice.

## Idle shelves

Tap the cookie, hire helpers and unlock the other shelves. All unlocked shelves produce together. Upgrades improve taps or production. A new volume awards permanent Story Stars after enough cookies have been earned and resets that run's helpers, shelves and upgrades after confirmation.

This is one shared game for this Keeper. Everyone who can open it can play and change the same progress. It requires no creator vault or wallet. Browser refresh does not reset it, and closing the page does not stop production while Keeper stays running. After Keeper has been stopped, catch-up is capped at 24 hours and settled once; moving the clock backwards does not repeat the same interval.

Idle progress lives in `shelf-idle.json` in Keeper's data directory. Home Assistant's app backup includes it. Keep that file out of public downloads. If it cannot be read, the game reports a recovery error and retains the file; it does not replace it with a new game. The rest of Keeper remains available.

Original standalone browser demos and the separate Home Assistant custom integration have independent saves. Installing this Keeper edition does not read, import, overwrite or uninstall them.

## Currency, sound and saved work

Cookies, Story Stars and battle coins are game counters. They cannot mint, mine, spend, transfer or redeem DVC or garden coins. Shelf Defense does not open vault keys or use an OpenAI key. Local artwork and audio are included; it has no streaming service or remote asset requirement.

Your private MozCorp bundle, Telephone projects, Studio drafts and existing Keeper identity keep their formats. Browser game progress is separate from the app's server backup, as are other browser projects.
