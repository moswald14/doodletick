01 / THE KEEPER

Your work lives here.

Write or open a text file. Drafts and your signing identity are encrypted with your password before they are saved in this browser. Export an encrypted backup to keep a copy somewhere safe.

Clearing browser storage removes the local vault. Keep the same local address and browser, and keep backups. Your password cannot be reset.

02 / THE DOODLECHAIN

A trail, one seal at a time.

Each seal signs a fingerprint of your writing and links to your last seal. Revisions become new entries. Receipts let another Keeper check the signature and, if you share it, the original text.

An isolated receipt cannot establish a complete history. Keep earlier receipts externally to help detect a replaced or shortened history.

03 / THE COLOR CLOCK

Time, with a little color.

Every RGB hexadecimal color gets one hour: 16,777,216 colors without repeats within a complete pass. Each pass starts red and ends green, then changes its visiting order. The wheel shows 24 hours of that journey. Ticks use UTC, starting November 11, 2026. A full pass takes about 1,914 years. Black focus changes the preview, not the signed RGB value.

The galaxy line is poetic. It is not a scientific countdown or an encryption key.

04 / ROOM TO GROW

A place to play together.

Enter the Doodleverse for a shared clearing, three games, chat, profiles, and a workshop for worlds you can keep and share. Playable worlds carry signed Creative Commons credits. Friends connect directly, and rooms appear on the lobby server you choose.

Global discovery, guardian recovery, cash payments, negotiated collaboration credits, and screenplay generation remain future work.

No global username reservation, anonymous network, or public blockchain is running. A signature proves control of a key; it cannot prove a soul or stop copying.

The Keeper locks after 10 minutes of inactivity. Anyone or anything with access to your unlocked browser can read your vault. This early edition has automated checks, but has not had an independent security audit.
