THE SHELF SURVIVAL HANDBOOK

A little field guide.

Enemies follow the marked trail. Put defenders on the empty pads beside it. Every escaped enemy takes shelf health.

Pencil Patrol
Quick, affordable shots. A strong first defender.
Sticky Situation
Slows enemies so your damage towers have more time.
Rubber Ranger
Splash damage for tightly packed troublemakers.
Pocket Knight
Short range, serious bonks. Build beside a bend.
Laser Lamp
A focused beam for the toughest targets.

Click a placed tower to open Tower upgrades. Every tower has a mini skill web: choose a main path, buy utility skills, and work toward mastery and a legendary final level. Green nodes can be learned with coins. Read skill effects to see what each node does. Bosses arrive on waves 5, 10, 15, and 20. Shelf battles keep running while you visit another. Use Pause all shelves to stop every battle. Reloaded battles start paused.

The Big Sweep hits every enemy on the shelf. Save it for a crowd or a close call.

MEET THE NEW INVADERS

Paper Moth
Flies over knight swords. Bring ranged defenders.
Inkling
Splits into two quick Ink Crumbs when defeated.
Binder Bug
Its shield absorbs damage before its health takes a hit.
Mender Mite
Heals nearby allies. Focus it before the group recovers.

Every defender has its own attack sound. Listen for shields, healing, boss arrivals, and victory cues. Use the speaker button to turn sound on.

Little tip: build around bends, mix your defenders, and keep a few coins for the unexpected.
